  content: The prestigious Cornwallis community is located just off the
    Caloosahatchee on Cape Coral’s east coast. Our canals offer direct
    (unrestricted) sailboat and powerboat access to the Caloosahatchee River and
    Gulf. By boat it is a very short run to the river and the open waters of the
    Gulf. Cornwallis Neighborhood Association (CNA) was formed in 1994 with its
    main purpose dedicated to the improvement and enjoyment of our neighborhood
    and maintaining all its spectacular beauty and surroundings. CNA membership
    is voluntarily and consists of residents, whether owners and or renters,
    seasonal and permanent, who are committed to and wish to enhance and
    preserve the beauty, fellowship, and safety of the neighborhood. Our
    activities, social gatherings, and general meetings are typically held
    October thru May. Our general meetings are held at Century 21 Birchwood
    Realty building located at 4040 Del Prado Blvd @ 7:15 pm. We encourage you
    to take the time to learn more about becoming a member.
